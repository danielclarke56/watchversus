Under $500 is where serious watchmaking begins. Seiko, Citizen, and Orient dominate this tier with in-house automatic movements, genuine tool watch specs, and — on select models — sapphire crystal. Casio's G-Shock sets the durability benchmark. Tissot brings Swiss Made certification at the top of the range.

The trade-off versus the $500–$1,000 tier is finishing: bracelet clasps are less refined, case edges less crisp, movements reliable but undecorated. These are aesthetic differences, not durability ones. The right pick here will outlast fashion trends and hold its own next to watches costing twice as much.
