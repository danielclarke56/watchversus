# Article Title (Full Question or Topic)

**Intro (2-3 sentences)**
Hook with a clear problem or question. End with what the article covers. Make the reader want to continue.

- **Point 5 (bolded term):** Explanation or finding (optional)

*This section should answer 60% of the article question in 5 lines. Reader who scans only this should have the main takeaway.*

## Main Section Title (H2)

Overview or framework paragraph. Establish why this section matters.

### Subsection (H3)

**Short explanation or finding.** 2-3 sentences max. Be concrete. Avoid hedging.

### Subsection (H3)

**Another finding or model.** Keep it specific.

### Subsection (H3)

**Another finding.** Parallel structure to above.

---

## Comparison or Breakdown (H2)

Use a table for side-by-side specs if comparing 2+ items. Otherwise, use short paragraphs.

| Attribute | Option A | Option B |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| Feature 1 | Value | Value |
| Feature 2 | Value | Value |

**Short conclusion.** Which is better and for whom?

---

## Pros & Cons or Key Takeaways (H2)

**Pros:**
- Clear advantage or strength
- Another strength

**Cons:**
- Potential downside or limitation
- Another tradeoff

---

## Alternatives or Buying Advice (H2)

For a review: mention 2-3 alternatives and how they compare.
For a guide: mention 2-3 sub-categories and how to choose.

**Example alt 1:** How it differs, when to choose it instead.
**Example alt 2:** How it differs, when to choose it instead.

---

## Final Verdict (H2)

**Conclusion in 3-4 sentences.** Restate the main finding. If a review, give a brief score or recommendation. Provide a clear next action (link to /compare/ or /watches/).
